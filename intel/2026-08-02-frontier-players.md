## Part A — The proven pattern

**1. Barbra Banda — Zambia, Forward.** Zambian football → Shanghai Shengli (41 goals in 52; 2020 Chinese Golden Boot). First woman to score back-to-back Olympic hat tricks (Tokyo 2021). Orlando Pride, March 2024, $740,000 fee — second-highest ever at the time (https://equalizersoccer.com/2024/03/05/source-orlando-pride-acquire-barbra-banda-740000-transfer-fee-second-largest-world-history-nwsl-salary/). Recovered from Aug 2025 adductor avulsion; as of late July 2026 led NWSL scoring with 12 goals in 15 apps; at WAFCON 2026 with Zambia (https://justwomenssports.com/reads/wafcon-2026-nwsl-stars-temwa-chawinga-barbra-banda/)

**2. Racheal Kundananji — Zambia, Forward.** Zambia → Madrid CFF (33 goals in 43 league games from 2022). Bay FC, Feb 2024, world-record €735,000 (~$787,000) — first African player, male or female, to break a world transfer record (https://www.foxsports.com/stories/soccer/nwsl-expansion-team-bay-fc-signs-zambian-forward-racheal-kundananji-for-world-record-fee). Bay FC all-time scoring leader since Sep 2025 (https://bayfc.com/roster/racheal-kundananji/)

**3. Temwa Chawinga — Malawi, Forward.** Malawi → Kvarnsvedens IK Sweden (55 goals/57 apps) → Wuhan Jianghan China (84 goals/84 apps; world's leading scorer 2023) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temwa_Cha%C5%B5inga). KC Current Jan 2024; NWSL Golden Boot AND MVP in both 2024 and 2025 — first back-to-back MVP in league history (https://sports.yahoo.com/article/current-forward-temwa-chawinga-wins-234607381.html); extended through 2029. At WAFCON 2026 scored Malawi's first-ever WAFCON goal plus a stoppage-time winner over Nigeria (https://justwomenssports.com/reads/wafcon-2026-nwsl-stars-temwa-chawinga-barbra-banda/)

**4. Tabitha Chawinga — Malawi, Forward.** DD Sunshine (Malawi) → Sweden at 17 → Jiangsu Suning → Wuhan Jianghan, loans Inter Milan and PSG (D1 top scorer, 19 in 21; D1 MVP 2023-24) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabitha_Chawinga). Lyon July 2024 to 2027; captaining Malawi at WAFCON 2026 (https://www.forbes.com/sites/assiletoufaily/2024/07/02/de-paris--lyon-tabitha-chawinga-signs-with-ol-fminin-for-3-years/)

**5. Melchie Dumornay — Haiti, Mid/Fwd.** AS Tigresses, Haiti (league top scorer at 15: 25 goals in nine matches). Spotted by Reims manager Amandine Miquel at the 2018 U-20 World Cup; Reims 2021, then free-agent signing by Lyon Jan 2023 (https://www.goal.com/en-us/lists/melchie-dumornay-lyon-womens-champions-league/blt88d81eca1bce2603). 2025-26 Première Ligue Player of the Year; led CBS women's Ballon d'Or power rankings (https://haitiantimes.com/2026/05/26/melchie-champions-league-final-lose/)

**6. Chiamaka Nnadozie — Nigeria, GK.** Rivers Angels (Nigeria WPL) → Paris FC Jan 2020 (118 apps; first African UNFP D1 GK of the Season, 2024) → Brighton free transfer July 2025 — "a statement of intent in WSL" (https://punchng.com/brighton-sign-nnadozie-on-free-transfer/; https://www.espn.com/espn/story/_/id/45640350/brighton-signing-nigeria-keeper-chiamaka-nnadozie-statement-intent-wsl). Six clean sheets in debut WSL season; Nigeria's #1 at WAFCON 2026

**7. Naomi Girma — USA, CB.** Stanford → San Diego Wave (#1 draft pick 2022) → Chelsea Jan 2025, world-record ~$1.1M, the first million-dollar transfer in women's football (https://www.cbsnews.com/news/naomi-girma-women-soccer-first-million-dollar-player-chelsea-san-diego/)

**8. Olivia Smith — Canada, Forward.** Sporting CP (low-visibility league) → Liverpool £200K 2024 → Arsenal July 2025, £1M — first £1M transfer, at age 20 (https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/45716362/arsenal-sign-liverpool-olivia-smith-world-record-transfer)

**9. Marta — Brazil, Forward (the original template).** Dois Riachos, Alagoas — barefoot with boys' teams; Vasco da Gama at 14; Umeå IK 2004 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marta_(footballer)). Re-signed Orlando Pride through 2026; best player of the 2025 Copa América Femenina, which Brazil won (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Copa_Am%C3%A9rica_Femenina)

**10. Claudia Martínez — Paraguay, Forward (the newest proof point).** Sportivo Ameliano → Olimpia (Paraguayan league). Joint top scorer at Copa América Femenina 2025 (6 goals) at 17 — youngest goalscorer and youngest hat-trick scorer in tournament history (https://copaamerica.com/en/news/claudia-martinez-youngest-goalscorer-copa-america-femenina). Washington Spirit, January 2026 — $950,000 fee (split $500K/2026 + $450K/2027 for NWSL threshold rules, sell-on clause), through 2028 + option, first Paraguayan in NWSL (https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/47672271/washington-spirit-sign-paraguay-star-teen-claudia-martinez-olimpia-source)

## Key patterns

1. The China/Sweden "data-thin middle leagues" are the proven bridge — both Chawingas, Banda, and Marta passed through them before record moves.
2. The record-fee frontier moved fast: $787K (Kundananji, Feb 2024) → $1.1M (Girma, Jan 2025) → £1M (Smith, Jul 2025); a Paraguayan-league teenager fetched $950K by Jan 2026.
3. The NWSL (esp. Washington Spirit and KC Current) now buys directly from zero-data environments — Zambia's league, Paraguay's league, Burkinabé pipelines — exactly the gap an analytics product covering WAFCON 2026 and the 2027 cycle would fill.
