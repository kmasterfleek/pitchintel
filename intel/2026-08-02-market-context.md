# Women's Football Market Context — research snapshot 2026-08-02

Mode A deep-research dossier. Every claim carries its source URL.

## 1. 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup

**Host, dates, format**
- Host: Brazil — the first Women's World Cup in South America. Dates: 24 June – 25 July 2027 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women%27s_World_Cup)
- Format: 32 teams from 6 confederations; the second and final 32-team edition before expansion to 48 teams in 2031 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women%27s_World_Cup)
- 8 venues in 8 host cities: Rio de Janeiro (Maracanã), São Paulo (Arena Itaquera), Belo Horizonte (Mineirão), Brasília (Estádio Nacional), Fortaleza (Castelão), Porto Alegre (Beira-Rio), Recife (Arena de Pernambuco), Salvador (Fonte Nova) (https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/womens/womensworldcup/brazil-2027/host-cities)

**Qualification state as of August 2026 — 13 of 32 spots filled**
- Qualified so far: Brazil (hosts); AFC (6): Australia, China, Japan, South Korea, North Korea, Philippines; CONMEBOL (2): Colombia, Argentina; OFC (1): New Zealand; UEFA (4 direct so far): Germany, Denmark, Spain, France (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women%27s_World_Cup_qualification)
- AFC: complete — direct slots decided via the 2026 AFC Women's Asian Cup knockout stage (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women%27s_World_Cup_qualification)
- CONMEBOL: complete — the inaugural 2025–26 CONMEBOL Women's Nations League (9-team round robin, Brazil excluded as hosts) sent top-two Colombia and Argentina through directly; Colombia won the competition after beating Paraguay 4-3 on the final day; Venezuela and Ecuador advanced to the inter-confederation play-offs (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_CONMEBOL_Women%27s_Nations_League; https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/womens/womensworldcup/brazil-2027/articles/fifa-womens-world-cup-qualifying-conmebol-review)
- OFC: complete — process finished 15 April 2026; New Zealand qualified; Papua New Guinea took the runner-up slot into the inter-confederation play-offs (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women%27s_World_Cup_qualification_(OFC))
- CAF: **underway right now** — the 2026 Women's Africa Cup of Nations (26 July – 16 August 2026) doubles as the qualifier; 12 nations, four direct World Cup places via the quarter-finals, runners-up to the play-offs (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women%27s_World_Cup_qualification)
- CONCACAF: groups done, finals pending — the eight-team 2026 Concacaf W Championship (Costa Rica, El Salvador, Haiti, Jamaica, Mexico, Panama, USA, Canada) runs November–December 2026 with four direct qualification places (https://womenfootball.blog/womens-world-cup-2027-qualifiers-qualified-teams/; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women%27s_World_Cup_qualification)
- UEFA: league phase done, play-offs pending — qualifying runs 3 March – 5 December 2026; league phase concluded June 2026 producing four direct qualifiers (Germany, Denmark, Spain, France); 32 teams enter play-offs (October–December 2026) for seven more UEFA spots (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women%27s_World_Cup_qualification_(UEFA))
- Inter-confederation play-offs: 10 teams for the final 3 spots, played November–December 2026 and February 2027 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women%27s_World_Cup_qualification_(inter-confederation_play-offs))
- Underdog watch: the Philippines qualified via the AFC route for their second-ever World Cup; El Salvador reached the Concacaf W Championship final eight; Papua New Guinea reached the intercontinental play-offs (https://womenfootball.blog/womens-world-cup-2027-qualifiers-qualified-teams/; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women%27s_World_Cup_qualification_(OFC))

## 2. Transfer market as of mid-2026

**Record fee progression (record broken 4+ times in ~20 months)**
- 2024: Racheal Kundananji, Madrid CFF → Bay FC, ~£685K — then world record (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most_expensive_women%27s_association_football_transfers)
- Jan 2025: Naomi Girma, San Diego Wave → Chelsea, ~£883K (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most_expensive_women%27s_association_football_transfers)
- Jul 2025: Olivia Smith, Liverpool → Arsenal, £1M — women's football's first £1m deal (https://www.goal.com/en/lists/arsenal-agree-world-record-transfer-fee-with-liverpool-to-sign-forward-olivia-smith-in-women-s-football-s-first-ever-gbp1m-deal/bltbc3febc13bcec137)
- Aug 2025: Lizbeth Ovalle, Tigres → Orlando Pride, ~£1.11M (https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/39523259/women-world-transfer-record-history-geyoro-overtakes-ovalle)
- Sep 2025 (current record): Grace Geyoro, PSG → London City Lionesses, €1.65m (£1.43M / $1.93M), set on WSL deadline day (https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/39523259/women-world-transfer-record-history-geyoro-overtakes-ovalle)

**Notable big-money moves, 2025–2026 windows**
- Alyssa Thompson, Angel City → Chelsea, just below Geyoro's fee (2025) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most_expensive_women%27s_association_football_transfers)
- 2026 windows: Felicia Schröder, BK Häcken → Real Madrid, ~£1.29M; Kerolin, Manchester City → Barcelona, ~£1.28M; Melvine Malard, Manchester United → Chelsea, ~£850K; Claudia Martínez, Club Olimpia → Washington Spirit, ~£703K; Sam Coffey, Portland Thorns → Manchester City, ~£650K; Kiana Palacios, Club América → Utah Royals, ~£600K (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most_expensive_women%27s_association_football_transfers; https://britbrief.co.uk/sports/transfers/womens-football-transfer-window-2026-key-moves.html)
- Marquee summer-2026 moves: Sam Kerr to Gotham FC, Yuki Matsukubo (2025 NWSL Midfielder of the Year) to Chelsea on a five-year deal, Ona Batlle to Arsenal (https://www.goal.com/en-us/lists/womens-summer-transfer-window-2026-biggest-deals-graded/blt678648c645cd29d1; https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/48991002/summer-transfer-window-grading-big-signings-womens-soccer)

**Aggregate growth**
- FIFA: 2024 global women's transfer spending hit a then-record $15.6m (https://global.espn.com/football/story/_/id/43625194/fifa-report-women-transfer-fees-hit-record-156m-2024)
- FIFA 2025 Global Transfer Report: 2025 women's spending reached $28.6m, up more than 80% year-on-year, across 2,440 transfers (+6.3%); 1,200+ clubs paid a fee, ~1,500 clubs earned one (https://inside.fifa.com/media-releases/international-transfers-reach-historic-high-2025; https://www.insideworldfootball.com/2026/01/29/fifa-releases-global-transfer-report-spending-hits-13-11billion/)
- Summer-2025 window alone: record $12.3m (£9.1m) global spending, 80% up on summer 2024 (https://www.insideworldfootball.com/2025/09/04/fifa-confirms-transfer-window-broke-records-womens-transfers-growing-80-2024)
- England (WSL) was 2025's top spending market ($11m spent vs $2.1m received), NWSL second at $7.9m; Chelsea the single biggest-spending club of 2025 (https://justwomenssports.com/reads/fifa-womens-global-transfer-report-reveals-chelsea-as-top-2025-spender/)

**Salaries**
- NWSL 2026 team salary cap: $3.7m ($3.5m base); 2026 minimum salary $50,500, rising to $82,500 by 2030 under the CBA (https://equalizersoccer.com/quick-update/nwsl-announces-3-7m-team-salary-cap-3-5m-base/; https://www.sportspro.com/analysis/womens-sport/nwsl-2026-valuations-expansion-revenue-hip-rule-trinity-rodman-marketable/)
- Trinity Rodman (Washington Spirit) signed a record contract worth over $2m annually (https://www.sportspro.com/analysis/womens-sport/nwsl-2026-valuations-expansion-revenue-hip-rule-trinity-rodman-marketable/)
- NWSL "High Impact Player" rule, effective July 2026: clubs may exceed the cap by up to $1m for qualifying stars (https://www.sportspro.com/analysis/womens-sport/nwsl-2026-valuations-expansion-revenue-hip-rule-trinity-rodman-marketable/)

**Market-value coverage (the data-gap opportunity)**
- Transfermarkt covers women's football through Soccerdonna (Transfermarkt-operated), which has systematically assigned women's market values only since 2021 (https://www.transfermarkt.us/morgan-sinclair-marta-amp-co-soccerdonna-establishes-market-values-for-nwsl/view/news/380452)
- Values are editorial estimates built from qualitative/contextual factors, not an algorithmic model (https://theriseofwomensfootball.com/womens-football-market-values-global-hierarchy)
- Top women's market value grew from €425,000 (Dzsenifer Marozsán, 2021) to €1.6m (Aitana Bonmatí) — roughly 4x in five years, still tiny and coarse versus the men's game (https://theriseofwomensfootball.com/womens-football-market-values-global-hierarchy; https://www.soccerdonna.de/en/womens-super-league/marktwerte/wettbewerb_ENG1.html)

## 3. Club investment climate

**Dedicated women's-football ownership / multi-club structures**
- Michele Kang's Kynisca Sports International (launched 2024) is the first women's-football multi-club group: Washington Spirit (NWSL), London City Lionesses (WSL), OL Lyonnes (France) (https://www.ol-lyonnes.com/en/actualites/michele-kang-announces-the-launch-of-kynisca-sports-international-ltd/)
- OL Féminin rebranded as OL Lyonnes (2025/26): dedicated women's performance campus, home matches at 59,000-seat Groupama Stadium; June 2026: Kang agreed to acquire a majority stake in Olympique Lyonnais itself (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250519576518/en/Olympique-Lyonnais-Fminin-Unveils-Bold-New-Brand-and-Vision; https://en.sedaily.com/sports/2026/06/24/korean-american-michele-kang-acquires-french-powerhouse-lyon)
- Kang pledged $50m to women's sport bodies plus $30m (2024) + $25m (2025) to US Soccer's women's program (https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/sport/football/articles/c134662m7jyo)
- Mercury/13 is a separate consortium building a women's multi-club group (https://ministryofsport.com/alexis-ohanian-buys-stake-in-chelsea-women/)
- London City Lionesses: the WSL's first independent (non-men's-affiliated) club, promoted for 2025-26, then broke the world transfer record for Geyoro (https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/39523259/women-world-transfer-record-history-geyoro-overtakes-ovalle)

**NWSL franchise values / expansion fees**
- Denver Summit FC paid a record $110m expansion fee (16th franchise, debut 2026) — more than double Bay FC's $53m; an Atlanta team for 2028 is reported at ~$165m (https://www.sportico.com/leagues/soccer/2025/nwsl-denver-team-record-expansion-fee-1234826002/; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Women%27s_Soccer_League_expansion)
- Angel City FC's 2024 sale valued the club at $250m (https://www.sportico.com/leagues/soccer/2025/nwsl-denver-team-record-expansion-fee-1234826002/)

**WSL / Liga F**
- Alexis Ohanian bought ~8–10% of the newly separated Chelsea Women entity for ~£20m in May 2025, valuing Chelsea Women alone at ~£245m (https://www.sportspro.com/news/chelsea-women-alexis-ohanian-wsl-investment-stake-may-2025/)
- WSL expands from 12 to 14 teams for 2026-27 (https://www.skysports.com/football/news/11095/13386295/fa-approves-womens-super-league-expansion-to-14-teams-from-2026-27-season)
- WSL commercial base: ~£65m five-year Sky/BBC broadcast deal (https://www.womenseleven.com/wsl-commercial-boom-broadcast-sponsorship-2025/)
- Liga F closed a €55m investment deal with a Pau Gasol-backed venture in July 2026 (€40m to clubs, €12m league, €3m image rights); Real Madrid and ~a quarter of clubs refused to join (https://www.sportcal.com/news/gasol-completes-e55m-liga-f-investment-despite-real-madrid-dissent/)
- La Liga relaxing spending rules: extra €2m allowance for academy/women's investment from 2026/27 (https://tribuna.com/en/news/2025-12-25-la-liga-to-relax-academy-and-womens-investment-rules-with-2m-extra-allowance-from-202627/)

**Scouting/analytics departments — nascent, thin, uneven (the gap)**
- American Soccer Analysis "State of NWSL Analytics" (Aug 2025): Houston Dash appointed a Director of Recruitment and Analytics (Jan 2025) plus a full-time Recruitment Analyst and three part-timers; Orlando Pride runs a Director of Scouting and Analytics plus a talent-ID scout — department sizes of 1–4 people are typical, versus 20+ in comparable men's clubs (https://www.americansocceranalysis.com/home/2025/8/5/state-of-nwsl-analytics-august-2025)
- WSL clubs posting women's-specific scouting/analysis roles as of mid-2026 — Liverpool Women (Assistant First Team Analyst), Brighton (Video Scout, Women's & Girls, £19.23/hr), Chelsea (part-time Player Development Lead & Scout, closing 12 Aug 2026) — junior, part-time postings illustrating how early-stage these departments are (https://jobsinfootball.com/jobs/first-team-scout-jobs/; https://jobs4football.com/job_category/analysts-recruitment-scouting/)

## 4. Why the next 12 months matter

**Precedent 1 — WWC 2023 (Australia/NZ)**
- ~2 billion cumulative viewers (double 2019), ~1.98m tickets sold; single-match TV peak 53.9m in China; $570m revenue — broke even for the first time; $1.32bn estimated economic impact (https://www.euromonitor.com/press/press-releases/july-20232/womens-world-cup-2023-viewership-to-cross-2-billion-double-from-2019-euromonitor-international; https://inside.fifa.com/tournament-organisation/fifa-womens-world-cup-2023-tournament-recap)
- Aftermath coincided with the market step-change: $15.6m (2024) → $28.6m (+80%, 2025) with the world record broken repeatedly Jan 2024 → Sep 2025 (https://global.espn.com/football/story/_/id/43625194/fifa-report-women-transfer-fees-hit-record-156m-2024; https://inside.fifa.com/media-releases/international-transfers-reach-historic-high-2025)

**Precedent 2 — Euro 2025 (Switzerland)**
- Record 657,088 attendance, 29 of 31 matches sold out; 500m+ cumulative global audience; the final drew 45m worldwide and a 16.2m UK peak; 21 partners drove +150% sponsorship revenue vs 2022 (https://gis.sport/news/womens-euro-2025-breaks-records-attendance-prize-money-and-global-viewership-stats/; https://wsc-sports.com/blog/industry-insights/the-womens-euro-final-sent-a-message-to-the-entire-sports-world/)
- UEFA July 2026 impact report: Swiss Women's Super League attendance +42% opening weekend, +50% across 2025/26; CHF 205m tourism impact (https://www.uefa.com/womenseuro/news/02a7-213454361e3c-561ed2b10cc7-1000--impact-report-shows-uefa-women-s-euro-2025-legacy-in-action/)
- WSL entered 2025-26 off England's Euro 2025 win with record broadcast audiences (https://www.forbes.com/sites/vitascarosella/2025/07/31/wsl-set-for-new-season-with-record-breaking-audiences/)

**The Aug 2026 → WWC 2027 window**
- Continuous qualification drumbeat: CAF concluding mid-August 2026, Concacaf W Championship and UEFA play-offs Oct–Dec 2026, inter-confederation play-offs Feb 2027, kickoff June 2027 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women%27s_World_Cup_qualification)
- Structural tailwinds peaking simultaneously: WSL expansion (2026-27), NWSL High Impact Player rule (Jul 2026), Liga F €55m injection (Jul 2026), Kang's Lyon takeover (Jun 2026)
- UEFA baseline forecast: European women's club revenue growing six-fold to €686m/year and fanbase 144m → 328m by 2033 (https://www.insideworldfootball.com/2022/08/16/uefa-report-says-womens-club-game-grow-e686m-revenue-2033/)
- Analytics infrastructure lags the money: fees +80% YoY, franchise fees doubling — while market-value coverage is one editorially-maintained volunteer site and club scouting departments are a handful of staff (https://www.americansocceranalysis.com/home/2025/8/5/state-of-nwsl-analytics-august-2025)
