# Ripple Sweep 01 — research snapshot 2026-08-02

First live Tier-0 sweep under the TERRA-INCOGNITA.md doctrine. Method: four
parallel deep-research agents, one per ripple family (bureaucratic/press,
social/bystander, WAFCON-region networks, pathway-edge/LatAm), public-record
sources only, every claim URL-sourced.

**Safeguarding applied:** players under 18 are written as [girl, age, place]
and never named in this dossier, even where sources name them — the source URL
preserves provenance. Adults and institutions are named.

Grades per doctrine: FOLKLORE (single source) → LEAD (two independent) →
EXPEDITION-GRADE (multiple sources + contactable context).

---

## A. The headline: the thesis is publicly admitted by the system itself

- Malawian press openly questions national scouting: 18 of 36 players (50%) in
  the March 2026 U-17 girls squad come from ONE academy (Luwinga, Mzuzu) "amid
  scouting concerns" (https://malawi24.com/2026/03/19/luwinga-academy-dominates-malawi-u-17-girls-national-team-call-up-amid-scouting-concerns/)
- Right to Dream (Ghana): girls were historically ~1 per 100 boys at trials;
  the academy just hired its **first full-time female recruiter dedicated to
  women's football in Africa** (https://www.tribalfootball.com/article/soccer-africa-cup-of-nations-women-exclusive-right-to-dream-academy-director-on-creating-top-women-s-players-wafcon-success-dc6ce3c1-0c76-41c7-83bf-fd678ac8ba2d)
- Soccerdonna/press: under-sampling is structural, not accidental. The
  under-scouted girl is not an edge case; she is the median case.

## D. Structural findings

1. **Brazil's futsal courts are the richest seam in the hemisphere**: ~88–89%
   of Brazil's female players came through futsal (state news agency:
   https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/esportes/noticia/2023-08/futsal-abre-portas-e-fomenta-o-futebol-de-meninas-e-mulheres-no-brasil);
   boys' futsal is the informal-formal seam, courts are forcing it open, and
   CBF's 27-federation girls' U-15/U-17 buildout ahead of WWC 2027 creates new
   records-edges every season (https://onefootball.com/en/news/with-the-2027-world-cup-in-sight-cbf-outlines-womens-football-in-brazil-43146737)
2. **CAF Schools Championship + CAF GIFT are the continental aggregator
   surfaces** where pre-pathway African girls first become legible — both
   publish school, academy, and coach names (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAF_African_Schools_Football_Championship ;
   https://www.cafonline.com/womens-football/news/all-you-need-to-know-about-the-caf-under-17-girls-integrated-football-tournament-gift-zimbabwe-2025/)
3. **The Chawinga/Banda/Tanzanite effect is at maximum amplitude right now**:
   Malawi beat Nigeria 3-2 at WAFCON (Jul 28); Tanzania is the first East
   African nation at a U-20 Women's World Cup (Poland, Sept 2026). Local-press
   harvest window: Aug–Oct 2026 (https://www.beinsports.com/en-us/soccer/womens-africa-cup-of-nations/articles/malawi-turns-its-debut-into-a-night-that-completely-reshapes-the-wafcon-landscape-2026-07-28 ;
   https://dailynews.co.tz/u20-world-cup-tanzanite-queens-qualify/)
4. **India — anonymous fan pages as discovery signal AND safeguarding
   hazard**: a viral schoolyard clip of [girl, school-age, Tripura] spawned
   fan groups crowdsourcing her identity (https://www.freepressjournal.in/education/heartwarming-video-tripura-school-girl-outplaying-boys-in-football-goes-viral).
   The disguise archetype is in national press: Manisha Kalyan toured village
   tournaments as "ten boys and a girl" (https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2022/8/19/manisha-kalyan-first-indian-player-in-womens-champions-league).
   Rajasthan NGO "Football for Freedom" (~800 girls, 13 villages, coordinator

## F. Negative results and method notes (as important as the finds)

- **No aggregator exists** for African/South Asian/LatAm/MENA girls'
  grassroots clips — aggregation happens via generic TikTok discover hubs,
  Spanish-language "virales" desks, and ad-hoc anonymous fan pages (the last
  being both the strongest signal and the worst safeguarding hazard).
  Building the safe version of this aggregator is an open product slot.
- **No verified 2024–26 case of a girl signed off a TikTok/skill challenge**
  — the mechanism is proven adjacent (Nigerian male player → Sevilla trial
  invite: https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/headlines/770824-how-social-media-power-earned-nigerian-footballer-sevilla-fc-d-trial.html)
  but the female case does not exist yet. First mover slot.
- **No mini-pitch program (FIFA Arena, Cruyff Courts) reports any
  talent-discovery outcome** — infrastructure exists, sensing doesn't.
- **Verification hazards**: gender misattribution (a viral "girl" who was a
  5-year-old boy: https://www.mdig.com.br/index.php?itemid=45073); staged
  creator skits polluting Arabic TikTok results; location conflicts even in
  press coverage. Corpus needs a staged/organic classifier and a
  two-source rule before any lead grades above FOLKLORE.
- **Search-language yield ranking**: Spanish virales desks > English local
  press > Arabic TikTok > Portuguese (noisy) > French (exclude the cartoon
  "Foot 2 Rue" from all French queries).
- **Coverage gaps for next sweeps**: Sub-Saharan ban cases, Mexico state
  leagues, Urdu-language media, Kenyan/Ugandan county press, Central American
  girl-in-boys'-league stories — go via country-level outlets, not aggregate
  search.
